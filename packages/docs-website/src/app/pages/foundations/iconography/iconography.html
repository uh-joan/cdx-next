Iconography principles

Icons should always serve a clear purpose. When used consistently and with intent, they enhance usability and reinforce understanding across our products. Helix iconography is guided by the following principles:

  • Purposeful – Every icon must have a functional role. Use them to support meaning, never as decoration.
  • Recognisable – Repeated use of familiar icons builds trust and reduces cognitive load—helping users move quickly through common flows.
  • Supportive – Icons should clarify content. They can break up dense information, highlight key points, or reinforce actions in components like forms and menus.

Avoid using icons when they don’t enhance the experience:

  • No decoration – Never include icons purely for visual interest or to fill space.
  • No clutter – In dense layouts, icons may distract or compete with important information. Only use them when they improve clarity.

Icon set

Helix uses the Google Material Symbols and Icons library, configured to align with the Clarivate brand guidelines and maintain consistency across the system. We use the following settings:

  • Style: Outline
  • Fill: 0
  • Weight: 400
  • Grade: 0
  • Optical size: 48

These settings provide a clean, lightweight visual style that complements both our UI and overall brand expression—ensuring clarity, legibility, and cohesion throughout the Helix design system.
