Typography principles

Helix typography is guided by three core principles:

  • Clarity – Type should always prioritise legibility, making content easy to scan and understand.
  • Hierarchy – A clear visual structure helps users navigate pages and focus on what matters most.
  • Consistency – Reusing standardised type styles across products creates familiarity and reinforces trust.

Typefaces

Helix uses two font families to balance brand identity with functional clarity:

  • Clarivate Regular – Our primary brand typeface, used sparingly to introduce character and reinforce brand presence.
  • Source Sans 3 – A clean, versatile typeface optimised for UI. Used throughout our products for its legibility and flexibility.

Together, these fonts create a purposeful, polished typographic system that works across content types and device sizes.

Type roles

Helix follows Google Material 3 Typography, organised into five functional roles: Display, Headline, Title, Body, and Label. Each role includes three sizes—large, medium, and small—to provide flexibility and hierarchy within and across layouts.

Display

Display

Display styles use Clarivate Regular and are reserved for large, branded text. Use them sparingly for key headings such as homepages, cards, or dialog titles.

Headline

Headline

Headline styles are ideal for short, high-visibility text. Use them to highlight primary content areas or key sections of a page.

Title

Title

Title styles provide mid-level emphasis, ideal for structuring sections or supporting content blocks.

Body

Body

Body styles are designed for longer passages of text. They prioritise comfort and readability across all devices.

Label

Label

Label styles are small and functional. Use them for interface elements like buttons, inputs and metadata.
