Branding principles

Helix follows three key principles when applying brand assets:

  • Purposeful – Elements like the logo, icons, typography, and pictograms are only used where they add clarity, reinforce identity, or enhance the user experience.
  • Usability-first – Branding should never interfere with accessibility or functionality. Clear, effective design always takes priority.
  • Aligned – All assets follow the Clarivate brand guidelines, ensuring consistency across products, platforms, and touchpoints.

Logo

The Clarivate logo is a core part of our visual identity and should be present in every product. Placement depends on the product’s branding context:

  • Use the logo in the header when Clarivate is the primary brand.
  • Use the logo in the footer when the product name takes precedence.

Both our and components include built-in options for adding the logo appropriately.
