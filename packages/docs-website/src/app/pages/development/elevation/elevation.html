Overview

Helix Elevation has been designed to provide a consistent and predictable visual hierarchy across the Helix Design System. Elevation is the relative depth, or distance, between two surfaces along the z-axis. Elevation helps users understand the relative importance of elements on the screen, and focus on the task at hand.

Helix uses three elevation levels: small, medium, and large. Each level provides a different degree of depth and shadow to help distinguish elements creating a visual hierarchy.
