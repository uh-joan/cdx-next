When to use

For app-like products, because it is more common to use a side bar as primary navigation. The experience is dynamic and feels more like using an app than a simple website.

  • High interactivity: Users often switch between different reports or modules frequently.
  • Complex navigation: They have multiple sections or tools that users access frequently.
  • Experience similar to desktop tools: They respond quickly and include menus, panels, and personalized options.

Use cases

Any app-like products, such as:

  • AI full conversation experience: Products where users need personalized, interactive, and natural language support to boost productivity and simplify tasks. For instance, CRI → full AI conversation experience.
  • Dashboards: Switch between different reports or modules, have complex navigation to access various tools or settings.
