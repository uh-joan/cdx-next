Before the user can talk to the assistant, they have to find it. Every app surfaces AI differently — reg-ai a sidebar rail, cmc a header button, off-x a floating action button — and none uses the Helix AI primitives, so each screen reinvents the look. This pattern settles where AI lives and how the entry wears the AI treatment, grounded in the 's Transparent principle. For AI scoped to on-screen content use ; the surface they all open is the .

The three surfaces

  • Primary trigger — a header button or rail (hlx-btn-ai). The always-there way in.
  • Floating action button — an extended FAB (hlx-btn-ai) with an accessible name, for busy headers.
  • Promo banner — a one-time, dismissible invitation using the AI gradient and an hlx-ai-avatar.

Do

  • Give one clear primary entry, marked with the AI treatment.
  • Label every trigger; give a FAB an accessible name.
  • Make the promo dismissible and keep it dismissed.
  • Lead every entry to the same assistant surface.

Don't

  • Ship a bare, unlabelled sparkle.
  • Reinvent the AI look per screen.
  • Make the promo permanent or interrupt the task with a modal.

One treatment, one destination

The header button, the FAB and the banner share the same gradient and sparkle, and every entry opens the same assistant surface. The promo introduces the capability once, then stays dismissed.
