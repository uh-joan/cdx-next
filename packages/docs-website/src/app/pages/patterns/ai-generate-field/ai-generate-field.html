Not every AI help is a conversation. Often the user just wants to draft or revise one field. A one-shot action proposes new text and lets the user accept, discard or regenerate, with an undo. No app in the suite does this yet — it's net-new guidance, grounded in the 's Assistive principle (and where the pattern sends one-shot actions).

When to use it

Writing or revising one field or block of text in place. An open-ended or multi-turn task is the assistant.

Do

  • Propose the text for review; make Accept undoable and Discard lossless.
  • Offer specific, named actions (Draft, Rewrite, Shorten, Fix grammar).
  • Show generating and error states.
  • Mark the control and proposal as AI; note it should be checked.

Don't

  • Overwrite the field silently, with no review or undo.
  • Ship a vague "AI" button with no idea what it does.
  • Give one take with no regenerate.
  • Lose the user's original text on discard.

Propose, don't replace

The flow is always act → generating → proposal → accept / discard / regenerate. Accept replaces the field (undoable); Discard restores the original exactly.

Example

Use the AI menu to Draft / Rewrite / Shorten the field — the suggestion appears for review; Accept it (then Undo), Regenerate, or Discard.
