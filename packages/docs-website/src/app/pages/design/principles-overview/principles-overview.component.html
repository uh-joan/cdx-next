Clarivate's design principles are the practical standards to guide design decision making. They reinforce the core values of the business and help design decisions move in a consistent direction. They apply to the creation of components, pages, interfaces and interactions. It reduces the need for designers to set their own individual standards of work and instead look to unify product design through a shared language in which to evaluate all output equally.

Clarivate’ suite of products are all geared at providing data, analytics and information to the customer to support targeted workflow and insights-based tasks. Customers rely on Clarivate to provide useful information in usable ways. Our product interfaces should be hyper focused on ensuring customers can retrieve information or complete actions as effortlessly as possible. It’s through that lens that we have developed the following key design principles:

“Helps me do my job better” (useful)


Focus on the customer journey

  • Consider the task a user is trying to complete and make the flow frictionless
  • Bear the burden of the information hierarchy and make the system, not the user, do the work
  • Reveal information proactively versus the customer having to find information on their own

Document the journey

  • Highlight points of pain and target improvements in the workflow
  • Pain Driven Design!

“I can't live without it” (desirable)


Satisfies deep emotional need

  • Experience triggers positive emotions like trust, confidence and satisfaction
  • Experience usage correlates to product value
  • “I get what I need so I’m okay with spending my time interacting with the product” and/or “it is NOT a waste of my time to interact with the product

Professional yet approachable tone

  • Expression and content is intelligent and appropriate for the audience
  • Use the user’s language versus business acronyms
  • Consistent tone and language across the experiences
