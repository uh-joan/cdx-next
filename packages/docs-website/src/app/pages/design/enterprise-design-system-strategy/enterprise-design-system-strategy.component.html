To maintain the integrity of the design system, these 4 tenets must be protected to ensure the protection and adherence to the enterprise design:


Identity

The enterprise design system must embody the brand's values, aiming for greatness in execution, inclusivity in vision and accountability in practice


Adoption

Must be a foundational expectation for products to ladder to the design system supported by clear communication and discussion for adoption


Adaptation

The system must acknowledge the complex landscape of existing and acquired technologies and provide transparency for what is most beneficial (yet maintain the strongest integrity)


Dedication

The system is a product in and of itself therefore a commitment must be made to evolve and maintain it
